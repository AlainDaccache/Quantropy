# Real assets, private markets and digital claims

An asset class groups exposures but does not erase contractual differences. A listed fund, direct property, private partnership and derivative referencing the same economic asset can have different fees, liquidity, taxation, governance and cash-flow timing. Classify the economic exposure separately from its access vehicle.

Real estate produces rent and incurs vacancy, operating costs, maintenance and financing. Net operating income excludes financing under a stated property convention. A capitalization-rate estimate V=NOI/cap_rate is a stylized income valuation, not a complete property forecast. **Example.** NOI 60 and cap rate 6% imply 1,000 before acquisition costs and entity-level claims. Rent growth, capex and exit assumptions can change value materially.

Private equity and venture capital involve capital calls, distributions, leverage, dilution and illiquid valuation. DPI=distributions/paid-in capital, RVPI=residual value/paid-in capital and TVPI=DPI+RVPI, with consistent gross/net and timing definitions. IRR is sensitive to cash timing; subscription financing and stale NAV can complicate comparisons. Public-market-equivalent methods need exact benchmark and cash-flow conventions.

Infrastructure and project finance depend on contracts, construction, operating risk and cash waterfalls. Resource investments involve depletion, reserves and commodity prices. Commodity futures exposure combines contract price changes, roll policy and collateral/funding returns; it is not ownership of an abstract spot-price index. Storage, transport and delivery restrictions affect arbitrage relationships.

Hedge funds describe vehicles and mandates, not a single return source. Diligence examines exposures, leverage, liquidity mismatch, fees, valuation, custody, gates and incentives. Structured products combine debt and options; principal protection is conditional on issuer solvency and contract terms. A complex payoff can be decomposed without being suitable.

Digital assets can be unbacked tokens, claims on reserves, tokenized conventional securities, or protocol-related rights. Analyze enforceable ownership, redemption, custody, key control, settlement, governance and counterparty exposure. Market capitalization does not establish intrinsic value or recoverable backing. Staking/lending yield can embed dilution, slashing, protocol and credit risk.

Collectibles, timber, farmland, carbon instruments and intellectual property need asset-specific valuation and market mechanics. Smoothed reported prices can understate risk and distort correlations. These introductions are not full specialist models; detailed contracts, waterfall calculations, appraisal adjustments and legal regimes remain openly tracked gaps.
