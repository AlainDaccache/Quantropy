# CFA coverage map and how to achieve completeness

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

Use the CFA framework as an authoritative coverage index, then add research and operational domains beyond its teaching scope. Its progression moves from foundations to analysis and integrated decisions. Include the common core and all three advanced pathways: portfolio management, private wealth and private markets. Practical skills also cover implementation workflows. [Curriculum](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/curriculum), [skills modules](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/candidate-resources/practical-skills-modules).

This is a topic-level crosswalk, not an assertion that every curriculum learning outcome is already implemented or mapped. The public 2026 Level I/II and three Level III outlines were reviewed; 2027 materials are available and need a version-delta review. Full lesson content is not reproduced here.

Completion procedure: create a reviewed concept/method inventory from each chosen edition; map every learning outcome to an original knowledge entry and, where applicable, a calculation, workflow or qualitative checklist; attach primary references and independent examples; identify exclusions and retail-access limitations; record coverage status and reviewer evidence. Broad topic labels alone cannot establish completion. Exact equations and accounting/legal rules require deeper source verification before implementation.

Extend the same traceability to premia/alpha literature, microstructure, historical data, software safety and broker operations. These extensions are essential for a reliable app even when they are not the curriculum's emphasis. The goal is original software/documentation informed by the curriculum and research, with a verifiable coverage matrix rather than a copied textbook.

## Coverage checklist

- CFA edition and source pinning
- Level I foundation concept coverage
- Level II analytical method coverage
- Level III common-core coverage
- Portfolio management pathway coverage
- Private wealth pathway coverage
- Private markets pathway coverage
- Practical-skills workflow coverage
- Learning-outcome to feature traceability
- Original examples and completion evidence
- 2027 curriculum delta review
- Beyond-curriculum research and operational coverage
