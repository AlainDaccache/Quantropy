# The Quantropy Quant Curriculum

*A CFA program for quants* — applied, executable, honest.

This book teaches the quant body of knowledge **by running it**: every code cell
executes against the [Quantropy library](https://github.com/AlainDaccache/Quantropy)
in CI on every build, so nothing you read here can silently rot.

Three kinds of lessons, always labeled:

- **`[deep]`** — real data, our own implementation (systematic trading, fundamental
  valuation, the anti-overfit rigor stack).
- **`[integration]`** — a topic taught by driving a mature library (e.g. QuantLib for
  derivatives), with honest notes on what retail data can and cannot support.
- **`[survey]`** — concept, a small illustration, and the references to go deeper.

The full syllabus lives in
[`docs/CURRICULUM.md`](https://github.com/AlainDaccache/Quantropy/blob/master/docs/CURRICULUM.md);
the verified bibliography in
[`docs/REFERENCES.md`](https://github.com/AlainDaccache/Quantropy/blob/master/docs/REFERENCES.md).
