# Valuation and business modeling

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

Valuation is a conditional estimate: what future cash flows and risks justify a price? Provide operating forecasts with revenue drivers, margins, working capital, reinvestment, taxes, financing and share counts. Tie income statement, cash flow and balance sheet together; assumptions must not create free growth without financing or reinvestment.

Core approaches are discounted cash flow, relative valuation, dividend models, residual income/excess returns, net asset value, sum of the parts and liquidation analysis. Separate enterprise cash flow (FCFF discounted at a consistent capital cost) from equity cash flow (FCFE discounted at equity cost), then reconcile debt, cash, non-operating assets, minority interests and diluted ownership. Show nominal versus real and currency consistency. [Damodaran course](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/valuation/val.htm).

Value banks/insurers through sector-appropriate capital and equity economics rather than automatically applying industrial-company enterprise multiples. REITs need property economics and maintenance spending; cyclicals need normalized scenarios; unprofitable businesses need survival and financing paths. Owner earnings estimates require separating maintenance from growth investment, which is often uncertain. [Berkshire letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1986.html).

Include reverse DCF, sensitivity grids, scenario trees, Monte Carlo with explicit dependence, dilution schedules, acquisition effects and real-option analysis. A probability distribution is not objective merely because it is simulated. Avoid reporting many decimal places while terminal growth or margin dominates the answer. Peer selection, accounting comparability and survivorship need scrutiny.

Acceptance: zero-growth and simple annuity fixtures; terminal growth below the relevant discount rate for the perpetuity model; cash-flow/discount-rate consistency; debt/equity bridges; scenarios reconcile; an output explains which assumptions drive value. Fundamentals may support discretionary decisions without becoming an automated alpha model.

## Coverage checklist

- Three-statement operating forecasts
- FCFF enterprise DCF
- FCFE equity DCF
- Dividend and residual income models
- Relative valuation and peer selection
- Reverse DCF and expectations
- Sum of parts NAV liquidation
- Banks insurance REIT valuation
- Cyclical and distressed valuation
- Dilution financing acquisitions
- Sensitivity scenario Monte Carlo
- Owner earnings and real options
