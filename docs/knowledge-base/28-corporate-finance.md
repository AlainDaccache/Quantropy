# Corporate finance, governance and qualitative research

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

Investment analysis needs both business economics and managerial incentives. Model projects through incremental cash flows, opportunity costs, working capital, taxes and financing. NPV and IRR serve different purposes; multiple sign changes can create multiple IRRs, and capital rationing or mutually exclusive projects complicate rankings. Evaluate value created per unit of capital rather than celebrating growth alone.

Provide capital structure, cost of capital, payout, refinancing and restructuring workbenches. Forecast interest, maturities, covenants, dilution and cash needs. Share repurchases can increase EPS while destroying value if overpriced or funded imprudently; distinguish accounting accretion from economic benefit. Acquisition analysis needs purchase consideration, financing, synergies, integration risk and impairment. Divestitures/spinoffs and business combinations need before/after balance-sheet and ownership bridges.

Qualitative research should be implemented through evidence-backed records: business model, competitive advantages, management incentives, capital-allocation history, customer/supplier concentration, governance, ownership/control and stewardship. Add environmental/social exposures where financially relevant, separating financial materiality, personal values and uncertain estimates. Do not collapse governance or competitive durability into an unexplained AI score.

Use interviews/filings/letters as sources with timestamps and permissions; extract claims for review rather than treating every sentence as fact. A thesis card should include counterevidence, unknowns, catalysts, downside and falsification conditions. Comparison views can show business narratives alongside financial scenarios without pretending all narratives are measurable.

Acceptance: a project cash-flow fixture reproduces NPV; financing does not appear twice in an enterprise valuation; buyback/M&A bridges reconcile shares/cash/debt; each qualitative claim links to evidence and reviewer confidence. Corporate-finance and qualitative capabilities are part of the library even when they do not generate a trade. CFA issuer analysis is the baseline; full decision models need separately sourced specifications.

## Coverage checklist

- Incremental project cash flows NPV IRR
- Capital budgeting and real-option scenarios
- Capital structure refinancing and covenants
- Cost of capital and implied expectations
- Dividends buybacks and value creation
- M&A restructuring spinoff bridges
- Governance ownership and incentives
- Competitive position and business thesis
- Financially material environmental social risk
- Evidence-backed qualitative research cards
