# What the owner is trying to build

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

The conversation points to a personal investment research and decision platform. The owner wants to stop switching among incomplete projects, learn unfamiliar finance, investigate any relevant asset or idea, build defensible models, test decisions, understand the whole portfolio and operate approved strategies reliably. The common need is a trustworthy path from a question to evidence to a decision to an accountable outcome.

A full-stack app is an appropriate delivery mechanism: a browser interface, shared financial services, durable data/state and background jobs. It should sit on a reusable library that is independently useful in notebooks and scripts. The app, knowledge base, research environment and trading operations belong to one product with different readiness requirements. The library should not require a browser or broker login for basic valuation.

Provide interconnected objects rather than a menu of unrelated calculators. A company links to filings, accounting adjustments, business thesis and valuation scenarios. A derivative links to contract terms, quotes, curves, calibrated model and lifecycle. A strategy links to economic mechanism, source paper, trials, data manifests, simulator assumptions and live evidence. A portfolio links to accounts, actual cash/holdings, goals, tax constraints, risk and performance. A decision links those records and later outcomes.

Two ambitions need separation: understanding the financial-market universe, and personally executing useful decisions. Broad knowledge can cover institutional/illiquid/OTC instruments through explanations and scenario models while executable profiles remain constrained by access, permissions, capital and operational capability. Knowledge coverage should not be narrowed by current employment restrictions; order generation must respect them.

The priority is reliable understanding and implementation of the owner's finances. A multi-tenant commercial SaaS, social trading network, exchange, ultra-low-latency market maker or fully autonomous AI investor would introduce separate product objectives not established here. Their absence from the initial build does not prevent broad analytical coverage. Commercial distribution could later require a new requirements/legal/privacy assessment.

Success is a complete workflow: explain what an analysis means, reproduce it, compare alternatives, reveal uncertainties, make a constrained decision, and reconcile its actual consequences. Adding another hundred indicators before the ledger is trustworthy does not accomplish that.

The owner has authorized broad research and development. This document captures inferred product intent, not a claim that budget, jurisdictions, first live instruments, hosting or commercial use have been decided. Those decisions need recorded evidence before they determine architecture or production enablement.

## Coverage checklist

- Personal investment research and decision platform
- Reusable library API and notebook access
- Connected company instrument strategy portfolio records
- Question evidence decision outcome workflow
- Knowledge breadth versus personal execution access
- Personal-use versus commercial-product boundaries
- Explicit product assumptions and unresolved constraints
