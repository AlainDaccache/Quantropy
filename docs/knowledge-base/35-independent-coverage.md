# Independent coverage audit and research boundaries

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

The earlier knowledge base was broad but weighted toward the conversation's examples: company valuation, factors, futures and options. That is a useful starting point, not independent proof of completeness. A source count says little about how deeply concepts have been checked. Most initial entries are domain specifications and research maps, not fully derived model specifications.

Triangulate several perspectives. CFA/CBOK identifies investment-analysis and portfolio concepts; FRM highlights risk, operational resilience and liquidity/treasury; CAIA highlights alternatives and manager diligence; CFP highlights life goals, insurance and planning; exchange/broker/infrastructure documentation specifies tradable contracts and settlement; empirical papers/libraries supply hypotheses and replication material. Technical-analysis frameworks can identify methods to investigate, without endorsing predictive claims. [CFA](https://www.cfainstitute.org/programs/cfa-program/candidate-resources/cbok), [FRM](https://www.garp.org/frm/study-materials), [CAIA](https://caia.org/explore-the-curriculum/), [CFP](https://www.cfp.net/for-education-partners/registered-programs/registered-program-content).

Audit across dimensions, not just topic names: economic domain; instrument/product; calculation or qualitative judgment; lifecycle; market/jurisdiction; investor goal; data availability/rights; research evidence; operational dependency; and user workflow. A chapter on options does not establish coverage of every exercise/settlement convention. A chapter on tax does not establish every country's rules.

The [coverage audit](coverage-audit.csv) assigns each domain an owner, describes current depth and lists the next evidence requirement. Every current row remains incomplete at detailed method level. Mark knowledge, quantitative implementation, simulation and live maturity separately. Deepen domain subregistries with exact model variants, original references, worked examples and limitations before calling a domain complete.

Important additions: investment-manager selection; market plumbing and financing; numerical/stochastic model validity; insurance/annuity/human-capital and life-event planning; technical/flow/positioning research with empirical controls; global accounting/legal adapters; commodity physical economics; benchmark/index design; investor protection and counterparty failure; data procurement/rights and scientific reproducibility. Many have already been named in earlier chapters, but naming them was not adequate depth.

No finite project can review all knowledge available or implement every institutional instrument equally. The achievable commitment is systematic, auditable coverage for a defined retail universe, explicit unknowns, and controlled expansion. Do not claim a universal percentage complete while the denominator of detailed methods is unresolved.

## Coverage checklist

- Multi-framework independent coverage audit
- Economic product lifecycle and workflow dimensions
- Knowledge model simulation live depth separation
- Domain owner and evidence-gap register
- Detailed method variant and source review
- Global jurisdiction and access boundaries
- Negative evidence and unsupported claims
- Explicit completeness denominator and review protocol
