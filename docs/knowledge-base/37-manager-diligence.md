# Fund, manager and product due diligence

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

A retail investor often buys access to a manager or packaged strategy. Analyze the vehicle and service as well as the underlying market exposure. An index fund, active mutual fund, hedge-fund-like product, separately managed account, private fund and structured note have different fees, control, transparency and exit terms.

Due diligence should record investment process, team/resources, governance, custody, valuation policy, liquidity, leverage, counterparties, conflicts, capacity, fees and terms. Review documents and version their dates. CAIA's public framework distinguishes investment-process and operational due diligence; both belong in a retail analysis workflow. [Curriculum map](https://caia.org/explore-the-curriculum/).

Performance review must consider benchmark choice, market/factor exposures, cash, fees, taxes where relevant, mandate changes, survivorship, backfill and reporting selection. A high return may reflect leveraged beta, option insurance sales or stale private valuations. Compare live and hypothetical histories separately. Track gross and investor-net returns without combining incompatible series.

For private funds/vehicles, model subscriptions, commitments, capital calls, distributions, fees, carried-interest waterfalls, redemption restrictions and side-pocket/valuation uncertainty where applicable. Add manager-specific evidence and legal/access constraints. Marketing liquidity may differ from liquidity under stress.

Benchmark/index diligence should inspect constituent rules, eligibility, weighting, reconstitution, corporate-action treatment, concentration and economic exposures. Track historical membership and methodology versions; compare total-return/currency/hedged variants correctly. Fund holdings can be incomplete or delayed, so look-through confidence should be visible.

Acceptance: documented costs reconcile with the fee scenario; exposure/attribution explains performance without flattering benchmark substitutions; missing diligence fields remain unknown; unverified operational or access claims cannot become recommendations. Choosing an existing vehicle may meet a goal more economically than building its strategy. The app should support that comparison.

## Coverage checklist

- Manager investment-process diligence
- Operational custody and valuation diligence
- Product fees terms and conflicts
- Live hypothetical backfill and survivorship review
- Benchmark mandate and factor attribution
- Private fund cash calls distributions waterfalls
- Redemption restrictions and stress liquidity
- Index construction and methodology history
- Buy a vehicle versus implement comparison
