# Ethics, behavior and decision discipline

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

An extensive app must help users reason honestly, protect information and avoid self-deception. Include source attribution, reasonable-basis research, fair performance presentation, confidentiality, conflicts and restricted-information controls. CFA's Code and Standards offer a professional framework; they do not replace applicable law or automatically apply identically to every personal user. [Current standards](https://www.cfainstitute.org/standards/professionals/code-ethics-standards).

Implement permissions for restricted assets, conflict disclosures and employment policies, plus provenance tags for public/licensed/confidential information. Avoid importing workplace materials into personal research. A restriction cannot be cleared by an AI model or a good backtest. Performance reports must state scope, gross/net basis, benchmark, fees and hypothetical versus live status; selective success screenshots are not a reliable record.

Behavioral support includes a pre-decision checklist, investment policy, precommitment, decision journal and periodic postmortem. Help surface overconfidence, confirmation, anchoring, recency, loss aversion, herd behavior and attention-driven trading through questions and evidence comparisons. A behavioral checklist assists judgment; it should not diagnose a user or fabricate a precise bias score.

Provide guardrails against result cherry-picking: retain failed trials, display drawdowns and adverse scenarios, and show costs/uncertainty prominently. Separate research excitement from portfolio action. User controls should support cooling-off/review policies where chosen without obstructing routine analysis.

Acceptance: restricted profiles are enforced in order generation; sensitive sources cannot leak into exports; reports preserve failed and live/hypothetical results; source claims remain auditable. Human judgment can be implemented as structured workflows with evidence, ownership and review. This is the appropriate answer to concepts that cannot be responsibly reduced to a formula.

## Coverage checklist

- Source attribution and reasonable-basis evidence
- Restricted instruments and employment policy
- Confidential material and conflict controls
- Fair hypothetical live performance presentation
- Investment policy and precommitment workflow
- Behavioral checklists and counterevidence
- Decision journal and postmortems
- Failed-research retention and honest uncertainty
- Qualitative judgment review and ownership
