# Household wealth and goals

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

Retail investing begins with the household, not a signal. Provide a balance sheet across liquid investments, cash, retirement accounts, property, liabilities and commitments. Distinguish investable capital from emergency liquidity and near-term spending. A concentrated trading account may be a small or large part of total economic risk depending on the household.

Model cash-flow budgets, debt repayment, savings targets, retirement contributions/withdrawals, inflation, income uncertainty and tax-aware account location. Retirement simulations need sequence-of-returns risk, longevity, spending flexibility, pensions and scenario dependence. A success probability is conditional on assumptions and distribution choices; show stress paths and shortfall amounts as well as one percentage.

Track recurring investments, rebalancing, distributions and a goal-consistent policy portfolio. Include fee drag, borrowing costs and the opportunity cost of active research. Basic debt and liquidity decisions may matter more than adding another factor. Insurance and estate planning belong as checklists and scenario inputs; jurisdiction-specific recommendations require separately verified rules.

Integrate account aggregation or manual imports before attempting to reproduce a banking platform. External accounts can supply holdings/flows at different refresh cadences and completeness. Do not promise real-time consolidated wealth when property, private assets or external statements update infrequently. PortfolioAnalyst offers a reference for some consolidated retail workflows. [Feature documentation](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/portfolioanalyst/features.php).

Acceptance: balance-sheet totals reconcile; mortgage/debt amortization matches hand calculations; withdrawals and taxes are timed; retirement reports reveal return/inflation/longevity sensitivities; modeled liquidity cannot be mistaken for accessible cash. This module should help the owner decide whether a trading strategy is appropriate at all.

## Coverage checklist

- Consolidated net worth and account aggregation
- Budget income expenses and liquidity
- Debt amortization and repayment comparisons
- Savings contributions and recurring investing
- Goal-based and retirement projections
- Sequence inflation and longevity stress
- Pension benefits and withdrawal scenarios
- Asset location and household concentration
- Insurance estate and beneficiary checklists
- Fees opportunity costs and policy review
