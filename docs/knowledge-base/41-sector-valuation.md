# Sector and business-model valuation profiles

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

Do not give every company the same DCF template. Separate economic model, accounting adjustments, valuation method and scenario assumptions. Multiple methods should cross-check an issuer, but their agreement is not independent evidence if they share the same optimistic inputs. Damodaran's author materials provide a sector/acquisition research map. [Supporting materials](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/Inv2ed.htm).

| Profile | Model focus and implementation requirement |
|---|---|
| Industrial/service firm | Operating drivers, reinvestment, ROIC, FCFF/FCFE and debt/equity bridge |
| Serial acquirer | Organic versus acquired growth; acquisition prices, financing, earnouts, goodwill, integration and returns on all capital deployed |
| Bank | Net-interest economics, credit losses, funding, regulatory capital and distributable equity earnings |
| P&C insurer | Premiums, underwriting, loss/reserve development, float, investments and capital needs |
| Life insurer | Long-duration obligations, lapses, guarantees, asset/liability scenarios and distributable capital |
| Conglomerate/holding company | Segment-specific values, central costs, cross-holdings, debt location, tax leakage and minority claims |
| REIT/property | Property cash flows, maintenance investment, NAV, financing and reported/adjusted FFO definitions |
| SaaS/subscription | Cohort retention, expansion, acquisition costs, gross margin, stock compensation, dilution and survival financing |
| Marketplace/platform | Gross transaction value versus net revenue; take rates, network economics and incentives |
| Commodity producer | Production/reserves, price/cost scenarios, sustaining capex, depletion and closure obligations |
| Regulated utility | Allowed returns, rate base, regulation and capital spending/funding |
| Distressed business | Capital-stack recovery, dilution, restructuring timing, going-concern and liquidation scenarios |
| Early-stage/biotech | Milestones, scenario probabilities, financing/runway and contingent project value |
| Asset manager/broker | Client assets/flows, fee rates, costs, market sensitivity and applicable capital/funding |

A serial acquirer must not forecast acquired growth while omitting the purchase cash/debt/shares required to obtain it. A bank's funding debt is part of its business, so an industrial enterprise-value bridge can be inappropriate. Insurance reserve/guarantee risk cannot be summarized adequately by one earnings multiple. A conglomerate needs claim-level reconciliation to avoid counting holdings twice.

The inventory includes DCF, dividends, residual income, NAV, liquidation, comparables, reverse valuation, SOTP, acquisition synergy/control and scenario/real-option approaches. Sector templates should share validated primitives and retain specialized inputs; never pretend the names alone provide accurate value.

Acceptance: one independently worked issuer per profile, linked cash/capital/ownership balances, explicit adverse scenarios, assumptions and sensitivity, and traceable differences between methods. Sector-specialist accounting/regulatory references and full model derivations remain completion gates.

## Coverage checklist

- Serial acquirer organic acquired growth and capital costs
- Bank capital credit and distributable earnings profile
- P&C life insurance liability and capital profiles
- Conglomerate holding-company claim-level SOTP
- REIT property NAV FFO and maintenance profile
- SaaS marketplace cohort dilution profiles
- Resource utilities regulated and cyclical profiles
- Distressed early-stage contingent valuation profiles
- Sector-specific independent issuer fixtures
