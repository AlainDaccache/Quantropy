# What Quantropy should become

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

Quantropy should be a personal financial research and investment operating system: a coherent place to understand businesses and instruments, test decisions, manage household exposures, and operate approved trading strategies. Its quality comes from trustworthy decisions and records, not the number of indicators. This knowledge base is a product specification and learning map. It does not certify the current code or supply investment advice for a particular trade.

The ambition is reasonable at the platform level and unrealistic as a promise to implement every model, every country and every trading style equally well. A universal optimum does not exist: speed, model fidelity, affordability, simplicity, breadth and maintenance compete. “10/10” should become measurable requirements for a declared workflow. Excellent daily futures research and excellent options market making have different infrastructure needs.

Five jobs need distinct interfaces: Plan household goals; Analyze assets and businesses; Research hypotheses; Manage portfolios; Operate trading. They should share instrument identities, versioned evidence, accounting and risk. Valuation estimates fair value under assumptions; forecasting estimates future outcomes; a strategy defines decisions; execution manages orders. A good valuation model is not automatically a profitable strategy.

Design the architecture independently of the laptop's existing projects. Reuse code only after a requirement, defect review and independent fixture justify it. Do not merge everything into one large script. One project can contain well-defined modules and integrate mature external tools.

Platform coverage is separate from the owner's trading permissions. Existing employment restrictions, account rules and personal investment charter need confirmation before enabling execution in additional instruments. Broad analysis capabilities do not imply broad authorization to trade. No automated code should infer that a restriction disappeared because a chapter exists.

Success: understandable outputs, traceable inputs, honest uncertainty, causal research, reconciled books, controlled losses and recoverable operations. Profitability is empirical and cannot be promised by architecture.

## Coverage checklist

- Goals and investment policy
- Capability and readiness boundaries
- Workflow-specific quality objectives
- Personal trading permissions
- Build versus integrate decisions
