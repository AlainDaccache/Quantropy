# How research becomes a complete usable library

Status: specification and research map; implementation not certified. Edition: 2026-10-05.

Maintain three inventories: concepts/methods, empirical hypotheses, and executable workflows. For each entry ask what an investor must understand, what can be computed, what data is needed, and what action it may responsibly support. The same concept may produce an explanation, a scenario, a calculation and a policy check; each has its own acceptance evidence.

Search within each domain using independent curricula/frameworks, seminal original papers, subsequent replications/critiques, standards and current instrument documentation. Include null/negative findings, revisions and practical implementation failures. Practitioner/vendor material can explain implementation but its commercial incentives and evidence depth need labeling. Investor letters describe a process, not an automatically reproducible strategy.

Empirical libraries can expand the signal inventory systematically. Open Source Asset Pricing is a candidate corpus with original papers and implementation metadata; each imported definition still needs exact construction, data rights, timing and net retail feasibility review. [Author university project](https://wisostat.uni-koeln.de/en/research/software-and-data/open-source-asset-pricing). Preserve disputed interpretations rather than declaring every replicated pattern either guaranteed alpha or false discovery.

Technical tools deserve coverage too: trend/momentum, volume/breadth, volatility, price structure, seasonality and positioning. Implement well-defined descriptive calculations and chart workflows where useful. Predictive pattern claims require formal definitions and independent empirical evaluation. A technical-analysis credential's topic list is not proof that a trading pattern predicts profitable net returns. Full-method review is pending.

Each method card must record exact formula/variant, applicability, inputs/units, historical availability, original reference, independent example, numerical tests, interpretation and failure modes. Qualitative cards require evidence, confidence, counterarguments, reviewer and falsification conditions. Strategy cards add selection history, cost/capacity, benchmark, risk and out-of-sample evidence. Workflow specs connect those cards to persistence, UI/API behavior and operational state.

Research completion for a declared release means every promised workflow has reviewed methods/data and explicit exclusions; all gaps are owned; source versions and evidence are traceable. It does not mean the entire finance literature has been exhausted. Freeze a reviewable release scope and continuously expand the remaining knowledge map without claiming unsupported completeness.

## Coverage checklist

- Concept method hypothesis workflow inventories
- Seminal papers replications critiques and standards
- Source provenance access depth and incentives
- Empirical signal corpus import and review
- Technical chart volume breadth research coverage
- Exact method cards and independent examples
- Qualitative evidence counterarguments and reviewer
- Release-specific coverage acceptance and exclusions
- Continuous literature revision and gap ownership
